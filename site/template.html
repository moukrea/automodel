Why you want it

Stop thinking about models and efforts.

1
Don’t pay for thinking you don’t needA quick question runs on Haiku or at low effort: faster, cheaper.
2
Don’t get stuck on too littleA hard bug gets extra-high effort, instead of a quick wrong fix.
3
Your prompt cache stays warmEffort changes keep it; a model change only when it pays off.
4
Extra context changes nothing“FYI…” keeps the pick in force for the work in progress.
5
Subagents and workflows tooEach gets its own pick: Haiku, Sonnet 5.5 or Opus; big jobs get parallel agents.
6
Your call wins[effort:x] and [model:x] pin it, until you hand it back.
7
Every pick is explainedautomodel why shows Jev’s odds and the reasons for each pick.
8
Our tuning, or yoursMeasured defaults, updated with every release; or tune every knob Jev sees.
